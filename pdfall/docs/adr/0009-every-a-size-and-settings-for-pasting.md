# Every A size, text documents, and settings only for what gets pasted

The company's forms (`examples\formats`, kept out of git) cover far more than the A3 лист and A4 специфікація PDFALL knew: СКД листи on A1–A5, СПДБ листи and СПДБ «Вироби» листи on A1–A4, in portrait and landscape, and текстові документи (first and later pages, СКД and СПДБ). They use the same title blocks PDFALL already read, only on other sheet sizes. PDFALL now reads, plots, numbers and pastes all of them.

- **Size.** A рамка is any A0–A5 sheet in either orientation (the table in `00-settings.lsp`), and it is plotted at 1:1 on the paper of exactly that size and orientation, by its canonical name (ADR 0008). Size never decides the kind.
- **A рамка needs a title block.** With six sizes in both orientations, an ordinary box can be an A size (old drawings have a 297x210 legend next to the лист), so a rectangle counts as a рамка only when one of the labels every form has (Дата, Аркуш, Аркушів, Арк., Масштаб) sits in its corner. A bare rectangle is no longer reported as a рамка without a марка.
- **Kind.** The title block decides it. The Дата head sits about 37.5 mm above the рамка's edge on every лист's 55 mm title block (new and old forms alike), 32.5 mm on the 40 mm first-page title block, and 7.5 mm on the 15 mm later-page one. A title block whose Дата head is 35 mm or higher, or which has Масштаб (old drawings, ADR 0008), is a лист. Any other рамка is a специфікація if a column head of the specification table (Поз., Позначення, Найменування, Кіл., Примітка: a real one may lack some) is along its top edge, and a текстовий документ if not.
- **Текстовий документ** is a kind of its own: `<марка> текстовий документ NN.pdf`, numbered on its own like a специфікація (Аркушів only on the first page), with no без заливки variant, after the специфікації in both combined documents. Nobody uses it yet; it is there so such a page is never taken for a специфікація.
- **Марка.** The name cell reads `[prefix] <марка>` with an optional second line on a лист («Складальне креслення»). A марка is one word, with or without a digit («1ЧП», «Лорем»): one word, because it begins every PDF's name and merge reads it from there. The usual марка, with a letter and a digit, is the first such word wherever it stands, so a prefix may be any words without digits, or none, and old drawings read as before (ADR 0008). When no word has a digit, the марка is the last word of the name's first line: the prefix is before it, the second line under it. Merge checks such a марка by finding the file's марка among the name cell's words, since a long prefix may wrap it onto the next line in the PDF.
- **Settings.** Exporting has no settings: everything follows from the drawing. Settings choose only what PDFLIST pastes (a лист of a standard, size and orientation, or a текстовий документ of a standard), the марка it offers, and what goes in the name cell (the prefix and the лист's second line). They are kept per user with `setenv`, so updates keep them and a drawing never depends on them: what was pasted is read back from the drawing itself. They are set in a dialog (PDFSETTINGS) that previews the chosen template and the whole name in one line, and whose «Вставити» button saves them and pastes at once.
- **Two paste buttons.** «Лист» pastes whatever the settings choose, листи or the pages of a текстовий документ, and «Специфікація» stays a button of its own, since a марка's специфікація comes with its листи. A separate «Текстовий документ» button with its own setting made the one preview jump between the two documents, and nobody uses текстові документи yet.

## Consequences

- The templates are generated from `examples\formats` by `make_templates.ps1`, which also writes the outline of each into `src\lsp\62-previews.lsp` for the dialog's preview. The forms' placeholders (`ДВНЗ "ПДАБА"`, `Група-`, a stray `н`) are dropped; the organisation cell is left empty for people to fill in.
- A custom prefix with a digit is refused, since it would be read as the марка.
- The dialog can't be tested by the console AutoCAD the tests run in; everything behind it (the settings, the name text, the form catalogue, the preview's lines) is a plain function the tests call.
- The dialog's DCL is written at run time, and every text is put in with `set_tile`, except two that `set_tile` can't change: the «Вставити» button's label (AutoCAD showed the DCL's English placeholder) and the title. Those two are written into the file in the PC's code page, which AutoCAD reads DCL in, after checking that a file written there keeps their letters; on a PC whose code page has no Cyrillic they stay English.
